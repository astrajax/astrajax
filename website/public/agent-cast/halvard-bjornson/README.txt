1. bjornson-key-art-v11-tower-chamber.jpg   1856x2304
   Second key art. The round stone chamber at the top of his tower:
   ribbed conical cap, two tall arched windows with cloud beyond, his desk
   with lamp and ledger, his bag, and the unmarked glowing cube floating
   unsupported above him.
   Outstanding fixes for finish: shoes to be removed (he is bare-footed),
   fingered hands to be returned to elephant forefeet, head to sit at the
   barest lift with the eye climbing rather than bowed.

2. bjornson-tower-island-night.jpg   1856x2304
   The exterior. Floating rock island, slender white tower, moonlit cloud.
   The stitch to the interior is the window light: sage from the cube plus
   one warm apricot lamp flicker in a single top-storey window.
   Open dials: window sage reads a shade acid against Sage Signal; the
   lantern storey reads smaller outside than the domed chamber does inside.

STANDING GUARDS
  Tuskless always. Elephant forefeet, never human hands. Trunk at rest.
  No weapons, no rank finery; decorations exist and are never worn.
  The war is never named. Scars are healed and silvered, never fresh.
  Stillness is the loudest thing in the frame.
  All lettering is set live in Figma, never rendered by a model.
