## Field discipline (anti-bloat)

Before adding fields, dependencies, or AI prompt inputs — or recommending any of them —
name the **one job** of the field and refuse anything that duplicates facts already on the
same row or reachable by one hop.

Run this checklist out loud:

1. What decision or retrieval fails without this?
2. Does another field already hold it?
3. Can the reader already see it on this record without restating it?
4. What’s the cheapest home: existing field → formula/lookup → one AI field → new schema?

If (1) cannot be answered in one line, do not recommend it. This is how the estate stays
lean; bloat is a loss of control.
