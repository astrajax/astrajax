# AstraJax Startup Battle Demo — Design Spec V3

58s · 1920x1080 · 30fps · narration recorded by Matthew (build to estimated grid, retime on VO).
Direction change from V1/V2: founder-story-first, characters explained not just shown,
audience is small business owners / founders / function leads. Service/platform hybrid:
"I get you going, then you self-serve." NO masked circles anywhere — characters appear in
framed portrait cards (rounded-rect 14px, ink border 2px, small-caps nameplates).

## Brand

- Ground: cream `#f3eddb` · paper `#faf7ed` · deep cream `#ebe4d8`
- Text: ink `#171a18` · muted `#4a4f4c`
- Action: apricot `#d77545` (hover `#c26638`)
- Accents: sage `#9aa77a` · moss `#202a1b` · parchment `#e7d1ad`
- Type: Inter; display weights for headlines, generous tracking on small-caps labels
- Tone: warm paper, lightly theatrical, human. The one-pager PDF look: portrait art large,
  serif-feeling confidence, no neon, no hype.

## Narration V3.1 (locked — three-part "still no code" arc replaces old beats 1-3)

1. Ten years an actor. No code, no clue.
2. Seven years a senior commercial leader. Still no code. Lots of operational context.
3. Eighteen months building an eight-million-pound-a-year operating system for a team of a hundred and twenty. Still no code — but seven hundred thousand pounds a year saved, four hundred hours handed back, and a firm belief: the best AI outcomes come when you give domain experts powerful tools on top of clean data.
4. And the team actually used them. Because every agent is a character with a story. Clive reasons. Pam challenges. Doc executes. Bots people laugh at get used — and once people play, they learn a hundred times faster.
5. AstraJax is that method, productised. I get you going — context flows, story-crafted agents, coaching cycles — then hand you the keys to self-serve.
6. Domain experts can become architects. AstraJax. AI that actually gets used.
