# AstraJax — How We Work

**Purpose:** answer the AIE brief question: *how does AstraJax use AI in the way the company itself runs?*  
**Short answer:** AstraJax is not just selling an agent-first operating model. We are building the company with one.

---

## The Principle

AstraJax is built around a simple split:

```text
Humans keep judgement.
Agents take the sludge.
Specialists bring the taste, story, point of view, and trust that AI cannot fake.
```

We do not treat AI as a replacement for human expertise. We use it to give experts more leverage.

That is true in the product, and it is true in the company.

---

## Cursor Is Doc

Inside AstraJax, **Cursor is our Doc layer**.

Doc is the action dispatcher in the AstraJax architecture. He does not decide the strategy. He does not own the judgement. He takes an approved brief and turns it into structured action.

That is how Matthew uses Cursor:

- Matthew reasons through the business, product, and adoption story.
- Critical ideas are challenged before they become build work.
- Once a direction is approved, Cursor turns the brief into code, documents, demo routes, and production assets.
- The output is reviewed, corrected, and folded back into the system.

This is the same pattern AstraJax sells:

```text
Reason -> Challenge -> Human decides -> Doc acts -> Review -> Improve
```

The company is being built by the method it teaches.

---

## Matthew's Role

Matthew focuses on the human side of AI adoption.

The centre of AstraJax is not developers. It is **domain experts**: the people who know the work, know the team, know the risks, and know what good looks like.

Matthew's job is to help domain experts and creative specialists move from:

```text
AI scepticism -> AI confidence -> AI advocacy
```

They are not becoming creative because of AI. Many of them are already creative. That is the point.

Some of the strongest creative people are also the most likely to be sceptical of AI, especially where there is left-leaning cultural bias, ethical discomfort, or a prejudice that AI is only there to cheapen human work.

AstraJax does not dismiss that scepticism. It redirects it. The core of what artists and high-judgement creatives do is protected because they create the thing AI struggles to originate: taste, judgement, emotional truth, point of view, and art.

That is why AstraJax wants those people close to the work early. As AI absorbs more manual, repetitive, non-human-touch production work, the people with genuine taste and judgement become more valuable, not less. Their job shifts from doing every piece of labour by hand to directing what should exist, what should be cut, and what will actually land with humans.

The goal is not to make every operator technical. The goal is to help them become confident architects of the systems around their own work.

That means AstraJax is designed around:

- trust before automation
- context before action
- plain language before technical theatre
- human approval before system change
- adoption culture, not just tooling

Matthew brings the commercial operating context: he has already built and launched this pattern inside a large direct-sales operation, with characterful agents, training hubs, sandboxes, leaderboards, coaching loops, and human sign-off.

## Tara-Lee's Role

Tara-Lee is a celebrated artist and acts as AstraJax's **Creative Director** for visual representation.

Her role is not decoration. Her role is adoption infrastructure.

She makes the system visible:

- agent personas
- character identities
- visual hierarchy
- role badges
- booth graphics
- demo assets
- memorable visual language

This matters because people do not adopt abstract systems. They adopt things they understand, remember, trust, and enjoy using.

Tara-Lee is not being made more creative by AI. She is already a creative force. AI gives her more leverage around the edges — iteration, translation, production support — while the original taste and visual judgement stay human.

The AstraJax cast is not a gimmick. It teaches scope:

- Clive reasons.
- Pam challenges.
- Humans decide.
- Doc acts.
- Composer/Cursor builds what Doc proposed.
- HyperAgent runs deployed agents.

The visuals help non-technical users understand who does what and where the boundaries are.

## David's Role

David acts as AstraJax's **Story Director**.

He brings the craft of a respected film director, screenwriter, and editor: structure, pacing, emotional clarity, and the ability to shape a message so people actually feel it.

That matters because AI adoption is not only a technical problem. It is a human story problem.

Teams need to understand:

- why this matters
- what is changing
- what is not being taken away
- where humans still have control
- why they should trust the system enough to try

David helps AstraJax turn complex architecture into a story people can follow.

## Why We Bring Creatives Close To AI

AstraJax deliberately works with artists, storytellers, and high-judgement operators because their core value is human.

They are not useful because they can manually produce every asset, sentence, edit, or option. They are useful because they know:

- what is worth making
- what feels true
- what should be rejected
- what will land with people
- what has taste
- what has no soul
- when technically correct is emotionally wrong

AI makes those people more valuable when they learn to direct it.

That is the same principle as Court Mode:

```text
The system gives intelligent points of view.
The human decides.
```

In AstraJax's own operations, Tara-Lee and David are not being replaced by AI. They are being surrounded by more leverage. AI can create options, drafts, variations, production support, and technical execution. Tara-Lee and David decide what is good.

This is the operational proof of the product philosophy: **give high-judgement humans better inputs, then return the decision to them.**

---

## The Human Skills AI Struggles To Replace

AstraJax deliberately leans into the skills AI is weakest at replacing:

- taste
- judgement
- trust-building
- emotional reading
- visual imagination
- narrative instinct
- leadership through change
- knowing what will land with real people

AI can generate options quickly. It can help draft, build, test, and summarise.

But it does not know what a team is afraid of. It does not know what will make a tired manager laugh. It does not know when technically correct is emotionally disastrous.

It also does not originate art in the same way a real artist does. It can remix, accelerate, imitate, and assist. It cannot replace the lived taste, risk, identity, and emotional judgement that make creative work matter.

That is why AstraJax is human-centric by design.

## How The Operating Model Works

In practice, AstraJax works like this:

1. **Matthew sets the human and commercial direction.**  
   What problem are we solving? Who is it for? What needs to be believed before it will be used?

2. **AI helps reason and challenge.**  
   Clive-style reasoning develops the idea. Pam-style challenge tests the assumptions. The human keeps judgement.

3. **Cursor acts as Doc.**  
   Once the direction is approved, Cursor turns briefs into build work: code, documents, demos, scripts, routes, and interfaces.

4. **Creative specialists make it land.**  
   Tara-Lee makes the system visible. David shapes the story. Their work makes the architecture memorable and adoptable.

5. **Everything loops back into the brain.**  
   Feedback, corrections, better phrases, stronger visuals, and proof points are captured so the system improves.

## Why This Matters For AIE

The AIE brief asks how AstraJax uses AI in the core offering and how it plans to run an agent-first business.

Our answer is:

> **AstraJax runs on the same pattern it sells. Domain experts keep judgement. AI agents take action from approved briefs. Creative specialists make the system human enough to adopt.**

This is not a conventional software company with AI bolted on.

It is a small, human-led, agent-assisted company where the most replaceable work is delegated to AI and the least replaceable work is protected and amplified.

## One-Line Version

**AstraJax uses AI to make domain experts, artists, storytellers, and operators more powerful — not to replace the human judgement that makes their work valuable.**
