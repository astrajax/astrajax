## The Thesis

**AstraJax is building the company of the future now: small, creative, commercially sharp teams directing AI agents instead of waiting behind traditional software delivery queues.**

The old software company assumed humans do the technical labour and domain experts submit tickets. That model is breaking. AI can build, test, monitor, and improve software at a pace no conventional dev team can match on cost or iteration speed.

AstraJax is not betting on that shift from the sidelines. We are **living it**: a non-technical founder shipped production software with AI, governed a real commercial operation with it, and is now productising the operating model that made it work.

> **We are building for the post-developer-bottleneck era.**

## What We Are Building

Two things, one flywheel.

**1. The product — Adoption OS for AI agents.**  
AstraJax helps domain experts — not developers — design, run, and improve agent fleets their teams actually use. Reason → challenge → human approval → dispatch → runtime execution → coaching → brain improves. Full detail: `docs/business/one-pager.md`.

**2. The company — an AI-native operating model.**  
We are not scaling as a traditional dev-heavy software house. We hire high-judgement, AI-curious operators, creatives, and commercial leaders — people with taste, emotional intelligence, and domain context — and coach them to direct AI build systems the way our founder already does. Agents take the sludge. Humans keep judgement, client context, and approval.

The product and the company prove each other. Clients buy adoption. Investors buy a new labour model with production evidence behind it.

Propose -> Challenge -> Human gate -> Execute

## Why This Scales Lean

**Lower fixed cost.** No conventional engineering payroll to scale before revenue. High-judgement generalists + AI agents + specialist review where risk demands it.

**Faster iteration.** People closest to the work shape systems without a dev queue. Feedback → fix → ship loops measured in hours, not quarters.

**Embedded AI champions.** The scalable delivery model is one AI champion per function, team, or work area — a founder or peer operator, not a central dev team. Champions test and improve workflows at the coalface, which speeds deployment and makes adoption culturally easier because users trust someone who speaks their language and shares their pressures.

**Repeatable method.** The founder proof is step one. AstraJax productises the coaching path: user brain → business brain → fleet → maturity → efficiency credit. Each client brain makes the method sharper.

**Better sales motion.** AI-curious operators and creatives adopt and sell the method better than traditional builders selling to people who fear AI.

**Dual revenue path.** Platform-led adoption OS + premium partnership for teams that want hands-on architecture and champion training.

## The Wedge (How We Land And Grow)

We do not sell "AI transformation." We sell a sequence with a low-friction front door.

```text
The AstraJax Household  →  Brain & Fleet Sprint  →  Partnership / Platform
(stay-behind OS)          (first brain + fleet)     (ongoing operating layer)
```

- **Who we chase first.** Commercial or operations-heavy teams (£5m–£100m revenue) where one or two operators hold too much context in their heads, earlier AI experiments stalled after the demo, and a commercial leader — not an IT function — owns the budget. Founder-led SMEs, direct-sales and field operations, multi-site service businesses.
- **Why us, not a consultancy.** The Household coaches a non-technical champion and gives them the tools to drive adoption in their own ecosystem, not a slide deck. The method compounds: every client brain we build sharpens the coaching path and the product.
- **How adoption scales.** Train one AI champion per function, team, or work area — embedded peers who test workflows at the coalface — rather than scaling a large central implementation team. In a solo or very small business, the founder can be the first champion.
- **Why it becomes a category, not a boutique.** The hands-on partnership funds and de-risks the platform. As the coaching path productises, delivery shifts from founder-led to method-led to self-serve — the consultancy is *how it is delivered today*, not *what it is*.

## The Unknowns We Are Buying Down

We would rather name these than have you guess. The bet is attacking them deliberately.

- **Repeatability beyond the founder.** Only Matthew has shipped to the founder bar so far. The core experiment of the next 12 months: can an AI-curious operator be coached to that bar, and how fast? This is the single biggest unknown — and the main thing your capital de-risks.
- **External vs internal proof.** Butternut was built as an internal operator. First paying external clients are in progress, not closed. We are converting founder proof into client proof.
- **Platform dependency.** We build on Airtable, HyperAgent, Cursor, and Vercel. Mitigation is portable, human-approved context brains that move across runtimes — but a platform shipping "adoption" natively, or changing terms, is a live risk we actively watch.
- **Defensibility timing.** The method is the moat; early on, parts are copyable. The race is to compound context discipline, client brains, and reputation faster than the category fills in.
